      Bonito 21″

      X-Rap 08 Crystal Shad on the lightest rod, twitch-pause. Hogy anchovy epoxy on rod two, burned. Albie Snax rigged and boxed for when they refuse hard baits.

      False albacore 25″, 4 per badge

      Same kit. Pink or anchovy epoxy, crank as fast as you can. Any albie you bring to the scale counts against the four.

      Bluefish 22″

      Poppers, metal-lips, Kastmasters. Wire or 60–80 lb fluoro. Chunk bait on circle hooks for the parked rod.

      Leader and knots

      15 lb Blue Label to start, 3–4 ft. Drop to 12 if you see fish follow and turn off. FG to the braid, Alberto as the backup. Loop knot at the lure. Wet every knot.

      Snax rigging

      Unweighted on the 4/0 twistlock, dead straight or it helicopters. Twitch and pause with slack. Check seating every few fish.

      Reading the water

      Tight birds over small splashes are hardtails. Loose birds over a lot of churn are blues — keep looking. Cast ahead of the school's travel, never run the boat through it. Glass calm and bright sun make them spooky; a little chop helps.

      Trolling

      5–7 kn through the water. Standard X-Rap blows out past ~5.5; use Magnums for 7. Run 100–200 ft back. Outside-turn hits mean speed up, inside hits slow down.

      Weigh-in

      8–10am and 7–9pm, Edgartown Jr. Yacht Club. Total length, jaw tip to tail tip.
